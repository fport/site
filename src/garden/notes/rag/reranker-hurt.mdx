The textbook says: retrieve 40, rerank with a cross-encoder, hand over 8. On
this corpus the textbook was wrong, and the numbers said so.

- **The headroom is real.** Recall@40 is 0.95 — the right chunk is almost
  always in the candidate pool. A good reranker has 17 points to win.
- **This reranker spent them.** MRR dropped from 0.690 to 0.514 and the
  Turkish slice fell furthest. It also cost two to four seconds per query.
- **As a "no answer" gate it was worse.** It caught 12/12 negatives — and
  flagged 12/42 real answers as junk. A 29% false alarm rate.
