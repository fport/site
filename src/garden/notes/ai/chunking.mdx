Tuning chunk size is the cheapest measurable win — but the thing that matters
is not size, it is the **boundary**. A 512-token chunk cut through the middle
of an idea is worse than a clean 1024.

## Try in this order

1. **Split on headings**, not on a fixed token count. Carry the heading chain
   into every chunk.
2. **10–15% overlap.** Keep the sentence that lands on a boundary on both sides.
3. **Contextual retrieval.** Prepend a 50–100 token line — generated by a cheap
   model — saying where in which document this piece sits, then embed that.

<Callout type="idea" title="Measured effect">
  In Anthropic's evaluation, contextual embeddings alone cut the top-20
  retrieval failure rate by 35%, together with contextual BM25 by 49%, and with
  a reranker on top by 67% — from 5.7% down to 1.9%.
</Callout>
