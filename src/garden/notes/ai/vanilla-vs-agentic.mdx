Agentic RAG wins on accuracy and costs real engineering effort in latency,
tokens and failure surface. The decision *is* that trade.

<Callout type="note" title="Decision rule">
  If most of your questions are answerable in one shot, use adaptive routing to
  buy agentic quality **only on the hard ones**. Running an agent over an easy
  question is the most elegant way to lose money.
</Callout>

<Sticky accent="amber">
  If you cannot measure the accuracy gain, the latency and tokens you pay are
  real and the gain is an assumption.
</Sticky>
