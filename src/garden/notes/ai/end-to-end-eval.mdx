Isolated evals audit retrieval; end-to-end evals audit **the answer the user
sees**. Both are needed, neither substitutes for the other.

## Two modes

- **Label-free.** No reference answer: faithfulness, relevancy, tone, toxicity.
  Cheap enough to run on every commit.
- **Labelled.** Ground-truth answers exist: correctness, coverage. Expensive to
  write, and the sharpest catcher of regressions.

<Callout type="gotcha">
  High faithfulness plus low answer relevancy means the model is telling you
  something well-sourced and beside the point. That is a **retrieval** bug, not
  a prompt bug.
</Callout>
