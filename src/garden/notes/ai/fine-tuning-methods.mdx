Training from scratch is almost no product team's job. In practice the choice
is **which parameter-efficient method**.

<Callout type="note" title="The current sweet spot">
  Fine-tuning a strong open-weight 8B-class model with a thin adapter and
  running it **alongside strong RAG** is where cost-per-quality lands best for
  production agents.
</Callout>

<Sticky accent="rose">
  An adapter does not replace retrieval. A fine-tuned model still invents the
