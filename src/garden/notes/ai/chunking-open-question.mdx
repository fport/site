Still unresolved for me: where the retrieval unit should sit relative to the
embedding unit.

<Row gap={6}>
  <Box sub="cheap, loses context" accent="slate">Embed chunk</Box>
  <Arrow dir="both" />
  <Box sub="better recall, more storage" accent="violet">Embed summary</Box>
</Row>

Options I keep circling:

1. **Embed the chunk, return the chunk.** Simple, and what everyone starts
   with. Breaks on documents where meaning lives across sections.
2. **Embed the chunk, return the parent.** Small vectors, wide context. Costs
   context budget fast.
3. **Embed a generated summary, return the original.** Retrieval quality goes
   up; you now own a generation step in your ingest pipeline that can drift
   from the source.
