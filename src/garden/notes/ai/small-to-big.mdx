Intuition: **embedding a big block of text is a bad idea.** The vector blurs
and the one sentence the question asks about drowns in noise.

The fix: embed the small thing, hand the big thing to the answer.

<Callout type="idea" title="What it buys">
  Sharper retrieval, and an escape from "lost in the middle": where a naive
  top-5 has one relevant chunk out of five, sentence-window retrieval shows the
  model almost nothing but relevant context.
