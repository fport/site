When naive RAG breaks it looks like a **model bug** from the outside. It is
almost always a retrieval bug.

## Two different diseases

- **Low precision** fills the context with noise. The model may see the right
  chunk and still lose it in the middle — *lost in the middle*. The answer
  comes out close but wrong.
- **Low recall** means the model never sees the material to synthesise from.
  The answer is either incomplete or invented.

<Callout type="gotcha">
  A retrieval bug cannot be fixed with a prompt. **Log the retrieved chunks on
  every request**, or you will spend the week tuning prompts against the wrong
  problem.
</Callout>

<Sticky accent="rose">
  More retrieved tokens does not mean better performance. Raising top-k usually
  costs precision and makes the answer worse.
</Sticky>
